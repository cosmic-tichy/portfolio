Cosmic Itch

ᛉ

Cosmic Itch spends months developing a sonic world for each set, usually as an excuse to buy too many books and disappear into some arcane subject.

Lately, he's gravitated toward a more punishing aesthetic, with distortion and formant filters conjuring scenes of primordial steel, vast emptiness and ancient curses. Along with his various drum machines and synths, he builds his own instruments in Max for Live, designed for manipulation on stage. He's interested in how techno can alienate listeners while simultaneously bringing them together in dance. He wants to tell stories and build worlds with sound.

Worlds that suggest horror but demand dance.
